Usability Evaluation of a Compound AI Tutoring System

Mar – Aug 2026 · Tutorus Labs · KRW 30M

Coding scheme designClassifier developmentUser studiesAnalytics dashboards

An AI maths tutor, evaluated on whether its replies teach or only deliver the answer faster.

What I didDesigned the evaluation framework, wrote the utterance-level ICAP coding manual and built the classifier for it, ran the sessions with school and first-year university students, and built the analytics dashboard.

What came out
  • Coded dialogue corpus
  • Research dashboard
  • Journal manuscript in preparation

Agentic AI for Personalized Career, Admission, and Counseling Support

Jun – Dec 2025 · KERIS · KRW 50M

Multi-agent system designFocus group interviewsDelphi validationField pilots

A multi-agent system that reads a student’s school records and drafts the counseling opinion, tracing every claim to the line it came from.

What I didRan the teacher focus groups, designed the multi-agent workflow, ran two rounds of expert validation and the Delphi study, and piloted the system with 30 teachers and 150 students. Experts rated the design direction 4.37 / 5 and automatic record ingestion 5.00 / 5.

What came out
  • KERIS Report RR 2025-3
  • Working prototype
  • Invention disclosure
  • HCI Korea 2026, first author

AI Copilot for Teacher-Augmented, Competence-Adaptive Learning

Jul 2023 – Dec 2025 · IITP · KRW 1B

Multi-agent LLM systemsLLM-as-a-judgeExperiment designRater agreement

A national programme on what an AI copilot hands a teacher. My share: conversational systems that assess without testing.

What I didDesigned and evaluated a multi-agent LLM debate chatbot that draws out a student’s argument and scores critical thinking from the exchange — its evaluator agents matched human raters at an intraclass correlation of 0.78 — and co-developed a self-determination-theory career counseling chatbot.

What came out
  • CHI EA 2025, first author
  • CHI EA 2025, co-author

Prompt designInterface designPolicy report authoring

What came out

AI-Based Early Screening and Prognosis for Landscape Tree Disease

Jul – Dec 2023 · SUNGHA · KRW 33M

Dataset constructionTransfer learningModel comparisonExpert validation

The clinical question in another domain: can a model catch disease early, from ordinary photographs of a tree?

What I didBuilt an expert-validated image dataset of Zelkova serrata, the species that accounts for more than half of Korea’s legally protected trees, and compared transfer-learning models with and without plant-disease pre-training, which lifted F1 from 92–96% to 99.45%.

What came out
  • Zelkova serrata image dataset
  • HCI Korea 2024, co-author

VR–EP–EEG–MRI Digital Biomarker Basic Research Laboratory

Jun 2021 – Feb 2024 · Ministry of Science and ICT · KRW 1.2B

Clinical data collectionMultimodal fusionEEG and evoked potentialsMRIEye tracking

Four dementia measures — VR behaviour, evoked potentials, EEG, MRI — fused into a single reading, with Hanyang University Guri Hospital.

What I didRan the VR data collection at the hospital myself, with patients rather than a convenience sample, and analysed the VEEM multimodal data it produced — including the VR–MRI–SNSB cohort of 54.

What came out
  • VEEM multimodal dataset, N = 54
  • Patent, filed 2023
  • Four papers drawn from it

Digital Biomarkers for Early Dementia Diagnosis from VR Daily-Living Data

Mar 2021 – Feb 2024 · Ministry of Science and ICT · KRW 550M

VR development in UnityFeature engineeringMultimodal modellingClinical validation

Early dementia screening built as an ordinary task performed in VR, because questionnaires bend and mouse-and-keyboard tests are not how daily life works.

What I didBuilt the virtual kiosk task in Unity — the six-step meal order the whole study rests on — then led the multimodal model, deriving hand-movement and gaze features from it and reading them together with MRI to separate 22 controls from 32 patients at 94.4% accuracy.

What came out
  • Virtual kiosk task in Unity
  • JMIR 2024, first author
  • CHI EA 2024
  • Two IEEE papers
