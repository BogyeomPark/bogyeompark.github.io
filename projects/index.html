Usability Evaluation of a Compound AI Tutoring System

A learner asks about a mathematical problem in text and images at once. Does the tutor’s reply teach, or does it just deliver the answer faster?

What I did. Designed the evaluation framework, wrote the utterance-level ICAP coding manual and built the classifier for it, ran the sessions with school and first-year university students, and built the analytics dashboard.

What came out. A coded dialogue corpus linking engagement to learning outcome, a research dashboard, and the final report. Journal manuscript in preparation.

Agentic AI for Personalized Career, Admission, and Counseling Support

Korea’s High School Credit System gave students far more to choose between and teachers far more to advise on. A main agent with student- and teacher-side sub-agents reads the school records and drafts the comprehensive opinion, tracing every claim to the line it came from.

What I did. Ran the teacher focus groups, designed the multi-agent workflow, ran two rounds of expert validation and the Delphi study, and wrote the final report.

What came out. KERIS Research Report RR 2025-3, a prototype piloted with 30 teachers and 150 students, rated 4.37 / 5 on design direction and 5.00 / 5 on record ingestion (CVI 1.00), an invention disclosure, and a first-authored HCI Korea 2026 paper.

AI Copilot for Teacher-Augmented, Competence-Adaptive Learning

A national programme on what an AI copilot should hand a teacher in a future classroom — analysis, generation, coaching. My share was the conversational systems that assess without testing.

What I did. Designed and evaluated a multi-agent LLM debate chatbot that draws out a student’s argument and scores critical thinking from the exchange, and co-developed a self-determination-theory career counseling chatbot.

AI-Based Early Screening and Prognosis for Landscape Tree Disease

Tree disease costs the companies who maintain protected trees, and it is caught late. The same question as the clinical work in a different domain: can a model see it early, from ordinary images?

What I did. Built an expert-validated image dataset of Zelkova serrata, the species that accounts for more than half of Korea’s legally protected trees, and compared transfer-learning models with and without plant-disease pre-training.

What came out. An F1 of 99.45% once the models were also pre-trained on plant disease, against 92.0–96.3% from ImageNet weights alone, and a co-authored HCI Korea 2024 paper.

VR–EP–EEG–MRI Digital Biomarker Basic Research Laboratory

Dementia is read from behavioral measures (VR), physiological ones (evoked potentials) and neural ones (EEG, MRI). Each had its own literature; how to fuse the four into a single reading did not. Run with Hanyang University Guri Hospital.

What I did. Ran the VR data collection at the hospital myself, with patients rather than a convenience sample, and analysed the VEEM multimodal data it produced — including the VR–MRI–SNSB cohort of 54.

What came out. The clinical dataset behind four of my papers, and a filed patent submitted as a project deliverable.

Digital Biomarkers for Early Dementia Diagnosis from VR Daily-Living Data

Daily-living performance is what clinicians reach for first in early dementia screening, and both ways of collecting it are compromised: a questionnaire bends to whoever answers it, and a mouse-and-keyboard test is not how daily life is performed. So the assessment became an ordinary task, performed in VR.

What I did. Built the virtual kiosk task in Unity — the six-step meal order the whole study rests on — then led the multimodal model, deriving hand-movement and gaze features from it and testing them against MRI-derived measures.

What came out. 94.4% classification accuracy across 22 healthy controls and 32 patients, higher than VR or MRI alone, published as a first-authored study in the Journal of Medical Internet Research (JCR Q1), plus CHI Late-Breaking Work and two IEEE conference papers.
