Tutorus Labs
KRW 30M

Usability Evaluation of a Compound AI Tutoring System

Lead Graduate Researcher · industry contract, Tutorus Labs

A learner asks about a mathematical concept or problem in text and images at once — figures, equations, tables, graphs — and an AI tutor answers conversationally. The study asks whether that conversation actually teaches: whether staged elicitation moves a learner from passively receiving into active, constructive, and interactive engagement, or just delivers the answer faster.

  • My role. Designed the usability evaluation framework, wrote the utterance-level ICAP coding manual and built the classifier that applies it, ran the sessions with middle- and high-school students and first-year undergraduates, and built the analytics dashboard for reviewing engagement labels beside tutor correctness and learning outcome.
  • What came out. A coded dialogue corpus linking engagement level to outcome, an interactive research dashboard, and the project’s final report. Journal manuscript in preparation.

Jun – Dec 2025
KERIS
KRW 50M

Agentic AI for Personalized Career, Admission, and Counseling Support

Lead Graduate Researcher · national policy research, Korea Education and Research Information Service

Korea’s High School Credit System gave students far more to choose between and teachers far more to advise on. The study asks where an agentic system can carry that load: a main agent with student- and teacher-side sub-agents that plan, call tools, and reflect, reading a student’s school records and drafting the comprehensive opinion — with every claim traced back to the record line it came from, so a teacher checks a source instead of trusting a paragraph.

  • My role. Ran the focus group interviews with in-service teachers, designed the multi-agent workflow, ran two rounds of expert validation and the Delphi study, conducted the field interviews, and wrote the final report.
  • What came out. KERIS Research Report RR 2025-3; a working prototype rated 4.37 / 5 on design direction and 5.00 / 5 on automatic record ingestion at a content validity index of 1.00; an invention disclosure; and a first-authored HCI Korea 2026 paper.

Jun 2021 – Feb 2024
Ministry of Science and ICT
KRW 1.2B

VR–EP–EEG–MRI Digital Biomarker Basic Research Laboratory

Lead Graduate Researcher · Basic Research Laboratory grant, with Hanyang University Guri Hospital

Dementia is assessed through behavioral measures (VR), physiological ones (evoked potentials), and neural ones (EEG, MRI). Each had its own literature; how they relate to one another, and how to fuse four of them into one read, did not. The laboratory set out to identify those relations with deep learning and to produce a clinically usable VEEM digital biomarker.

  • My role. Ran the VR data collection at the hospital myself, with patients rather than a convenience sample, and analysed the VEEM multimodal data that came out of it — VR interaction logs, eye tracking, evoked potentials, EEG and MRI — including the VR–MRI–SNSB cohort of 54 participants. Wrote sections of the annual and final reports.
  • What came out. The clinical dataset behind four of my papers, and a filed patent submitted as a project deliverable.

Mar 2021 – Feb 2024
Ministry of Science and ICT
KRW 550M

Digital Biomarkers for Early Dementia Diagnosis from VR Daily-Living Data

Lead Graduate Researcher · Excellent Young Researcher grant

Daily-living performance is the measure clinicians reach for first in early dementia screening, and both ways of collecting it are compromised: a questionnaire bends to whoever answers it, and a computer-based test run through a mouse and keyboard is not how daily life is actually performed. The project built the assessment as an ordinary task carried out naturally in VR, then tested whether deep learning on that behavior screens as well as the clinical measures do.

  • My role. Built the virtual kiosk task in Unity — the six-step meal order participants carry out in VR, and the instrument the whole study rests on — then led the development and validation of the multimodal model, derived the hand-movement and gaze features from that task, and tested them against MRI-derived measures in a clinical sample.
  • What came out. A first-authored validation study in the Journal of Medical Internet Research (JCR Q1, impact factor 8.2), CHI Late-Breaking Work, and two IEEE conference papers.
