May 2025Poster

Three Late-Breaking Work papers at CHI 2025, including my own on assessing critical thinking

“Assessing Critical Thinking through a Multi-Agent LLM-Based Debate Chatbot,” Late-Breaking Work at CHI 2025 in Yokohama (32.7% acceptance; 619/1,888). Separate evaluator agents matched human raters at an intraclass correlation of 0.78, agreeing within one point 97.37% of the time. Co-author on two further CHI 2025 abstracts.

Feb 2025Award

2024

Dec 2024Award

Best Student Paper Bronze Award, IEEE Seoul Section

“Enhancing Early MCI Detection Through Integrative Approaches,” at the 2024 IEEE Seoul Section Student Paper Contest. VR behavior, MRI structure and neuropsychological testing read as complementary evidence rather than competing tests.

Jul 2024Talk

ITC-CSCC 2024: reading VR behavior and clinical tests as one picture

Oral presentation on integrating VR, MRI and neuropsychological insights: technology-mediated behavioral measures and established clinical measures belong in the same reading, not one diagnostic source at a time.

May 2024Poster

CHI 2024: VR and MRI biomarkers screen better together than apart

Late-Breaking Work at CHI 2024 in Honolulu. Behavioral biomarkers from a virtual kiosk task and structural MRI biomarkers carry different evidence, and together they screened more accurately than either alone.

Apr 2024Publication

Co-first-authored JMIR paper reaches 94.4% accuracy in early MCI detection

Published in the Journal of Medical Internet Research (JCR Q1, 96th percentile), with me as co-first author alongside Yuwon Kim. Across 54 participants, VR behavioral biomarkers combined with MRI reached 94.4% accuracy and 100% sensitivity.

Feb 2024Talk

HCI Korea 2024: catching disease in protected trees from ordinary photographs

“Artificial Intelligence-Based Heritage Tree Disease Diagnosis,” with Sabin Lee, Daejung Kim and Kyoungwon Seo. Heritage trees are protected by law, but the monitoring that protection assumes is expensive enough that trees are lost every year to disease caught too late.

Jan 2024Talk

ICEIC 2024: four ways of looking for early cognitive impairment, read together

“Early Screening of Mild Cognitive Impairment Using Multimodal Digital Biomarkers,” with Se Young Kim and colleagues. A virtual task shows behavior, evoked potentials and EEG show electrical response, MRI shows structure — and each sees something the others miss.

2023

Feb 2023Talk

HCI Korea 2023: two ways of narrating a health result, and why both help

“Counterfactual vs. Prefactual,” my first first-authored paper, presented under my former name, Hyobin Park. An MRI report says what was found, not what to do about it — looking backwards and looking forwards both made the result actionable, by different mechanisms.
