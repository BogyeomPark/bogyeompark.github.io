2026

Jul 2026Talk

AIED 2026: closing the ‘cognitive last mile’ between AI answers and a teacher’s decision

I gave an oral presentation, “Closing the ‘Cognitive Last Mile’: How Agentic AI Empowers Teachers’ Self-Efficacy in Career Counseling,” at the HAI-Agency Workshop at AIED 2026. The talk took up the gap between receiving AI-generated information and turning it into an informed counseling action, and how an agentic system can support a teacher’s reasoning across it.

Jan 2026Talk

HCI Korea 2026: a career-counseling agent built from what teachers asked for

I gave an oral presentation, “From Teacher Needs to Agentic AI: Designing and Validating a Personalized Career Counseling System,” one of eight projects our lab presented at HCI Korea 2026. The system starts from what teachers said they needed, and moves past information retrieval to organize reasoning steps, seek what is missing, and support personalized guidance.

Selected for the inaugural AI SeoulTech Graduate Scholarship

I was selected as a doctoral-track recipient of the AI SeoulTech Graduate Scholarship, which provides KRW 10,000,000 in research and study support. The Seoul Scholarship Foundation established the program for graduate researchers in artificial intelligence, and six SeoulTech HAI Lab researchers were chosen for its inaugural cohort — three doctoral, three master’s.

May 2025Poster

Three papers at CHI 2025, including my work on assessing critical thinking

I presented “Assessing Critical Thinking through a Multi-Agent LLM-Based Debate Chatbot” in the Late-Breaking Work program at CHI 2025 in Yokohama, which accepted 32.7% of submissions (619 of 1,888). Separate evaluator agents scored debates at an intraclass correlation of 0.78 with human evaluators, agreeing within one point 97.37% of the time. I was also a co-author on two further CHI 2025 extended abstracts, on self-disclosing chatbots for academic-stress assessment and on an SDT-based career-counseling chatbot.

Feb 2025Award

Best Paper Award at HCI Korea 2025

“The Impact of Self-Disclosing Chatbots for Academic Stress Assessment on Student Self-Reflection,” written with Minyoung Park and Kyoungwon Seo, received a Best Paper Award at HCI Korea 2025. The study asks whether a chatbot disclosing something of its own changes how openly students report academic stress, and how they reflect afterwards.

2024

Dec 2024Award

Best Student Paper Bronze Award, IEEE Seoul Section

“Enhancing Early MCI Detection Through Integrative Approaches: The Role of VR, MRI, and Neuropsychological Testing” received the Best Student Paper Bronze Award at the 2024 IEEE Seoul Section Student Paper Contest. Written with Jinseok Park, Hojin Choi, Hokyoung Ryu, and Kyoungwon Seo, it treats VR behavior, MRI structure, and neuropsychological testing as complementary evidence rather than competing tests.

Jul 2024Talk

ITC-CSCC 2024: reading VR behavior and clinical tests as one picture

I gave an oral presentation, “Advancing Mild Cognitive Impairment Detection: Integrating VR, MRI, and Neuropsychological Insights for Comprehensive Diagnosis,” at ITC-CSCC 2024, arguing that technology-mediated behavioral measures and established clinical measures should be read together rather than one diagnostic source at a time.

May 2024Poster

CHI 2024: VR and MRI biomarkers screen better together than apart

I presented “Exploring the Multimodal Integration of VR and MRI Biomarkers for Enhanced Early Detection of Mild Cognitive Impairment” as Late-Breaking Work at CHI 2024 in Honolulu. Behavioral biomarkers from a virtual kiosk task and structural MRI biomarkers carry different evidence, and combining them screened more accurately than either alone.

Apr 2024Publication

Co-first-authored JMIR paper reaches 94.4% accuracy in early MCI detection

Our validation study integrating VR and MRI biomarkers for early detection of mild cognitive impairment was published in the Journal of Medical Internet Research (JCR Q1, top 3%), with me as co-first author alongside Yuwon Kim. Across 54 participants, combining VR-derived behavioral biomarkers with MRI biomarkers reached 94.4% accuracy and 100% sensitivity.
