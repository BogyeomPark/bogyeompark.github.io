2026

Jul 2026Oral Presentation

Presented agentic AI research at the HAI-Agency@AIED 2026 Workshop

At the HAI-Agency Workshop at AIED 2026, I gave an oral presentation titled “Closing the 'Cognitive Last Mile': How Agentic AI Empowers Teachers' Self-Efficacy in Career Counseling.” The workshop brought together research on human-AI agency in educational settings.

My talk focused on the gap between receiving AI-generated information and turning it into an informed counseling action. I presented our approach to designing an agentic AI system that supports teachers' reasoning and use of additional information during career counseling.

Jan 2026Oral Presentation

Presented a personalized career-counseling agent at HCI Korea 2026

Our lab presented eight research projects at HCI Korea 2026. I gave an oral presentation titled “From Teacher Needs to Agentic AI: Designing and Validating a Personalized Career Counseling System.”

The work started from the practical needs of teachers and examined how an agentic AI system could go beyond information retrieval to organize reasoning steps, seek relevant information, and support personalized career and academic guidance.

May 2025Poster Presentation

Presented multi-agent debate chatbot research at CHI 2025

At CHI 2025 in Yokohama, I presented “Assessing Critical Thinking through a Multi-Agent LLM-Based Debate Chatbot” in the Late-Breaking Work program and discussed the project with researchers during the poster session.

Feb 2025Award

Co-authored paper received a Best Paper Award at HCI Korea 2025

Our paper, “The Impact of Self-Disclosing Chatbots for Academic Stress Assessment on Student Self-Reflection,” received a Best Paper Award at HCI Korea 2025. The paper was co-authored by Minyoung Park, Bogyeom Park, and Kyoungwon Seo.

The study examined how a chatbot's self-disclosure may influence students' engagement with academic-stress assessment and their subsequent self-reflection. This entry records the paper-level award; I am listed as a co-author on the certificate.

2024

Dec 2024Award

Received the IEEE Seoul Section Best Student Paper Bronze Award

My paper, “Enhancing Early MCI Detection Through Integrative Approaches: The Role of VR, MRI, and Neuropsychological Testing,” received the Best Student Paper Bronze Award at the 2024 IEEE Seoul Section Student Paper Contest.

The work examined how behavioral evidence from VR tasks, structural evidence from MRI, and conventional neuropsychological testing can contribute complementary information for early MCI detection. The paper was co-authored with Jinseok Park, Hojin Choi, Hokyoung Ryu, and Kyoungwon Seo.

Jul 2024Oral Presentation

Presented multimodal MCI detection research at ITC-CSCC 2024

At ITC-CSCC 2024, I gave an oral presentation titled “Advancing Mild Cognitive Impairment Detection: Integrating VR, MRI, and Neuropsychological Insights for Comprehensive Diagnosis.”

The presentation extended our multimodal biomarker research by considering how technology-mediated behavioral measures and established clinical measures can be interpreted together, rather than treating each diagnostic source in isolation.

May 2024Poster Presentation

Presented VR and MRI biomarker research at CHI 2024

At CHI 2024 in Honolulu, I presented our Late-Breaking Work, “Exploring the Multimodal Integration of VR and MRI Biomarkers for Enhanced Early Detection of Mild Cognitive Impairment.”

The poster reported how behavioral biomarkers captured during a virtual kiosk task and structural MRI biomarkers provide different but complementary evidence. Their integration produced stronger screening performance than either modality alone.

Apr 2024Publication

Multimodal biomarker study published in JMIR

Our validation study integrating VR and MRI biomarkers for early detection of mild cognitive impairment was published in the Journal of Medical Internet Research. I contributed as a co-first author alongside Yuwon Kim.

The study involved 54 participants and showed that integrating VR-derived behavioral biomarkers with MRI biomarkers improved classification performance, reaching 94.4% accuracy and 100% sensitivity. Project page →
