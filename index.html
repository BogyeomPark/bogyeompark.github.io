AI in Education · Human–AI Learning

Designing AI tutors for deeper cognitive engagement.

I am Bogyeom Park, an Integrated Ph.D. student in the Human-centered Artificial Intelligence Lab at Seoul National University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Kyoungwon Seo. My research examines how the design of AI-supported learning environments shapes students' cognitive engagement, learning processes, and outcomes.

?

How can an AI tutor move beyond simply giving correct answers and instead elicit explanation, comparison, reflection, and meaningful dialogue?

Research Directions

01

AI-Supported Learning

Designing and evaluating AI tutoring interactions that support meaningful student learning.

02

Cognitive Engagement

Examining how learners engage with AI through the ICAP framework and dialogue evidence.

03

Learning Analytics

Using conversational and behavioral traces to understand learning processes and outcomes.

Current Project

ICAP-Based AI Tutoring

From answer delivery to constructive learning dialogue

This research compares a conventional chatbot with an ICAP-based AI tutor designed to elicit active, constructive, and interactive forms of engagement in probability and statistics learning.
