Human-Centered Agentic AI

I am Bogyeom Park, a Ph.D. student at SeoulTech’s Human-centered Artificial Intelligence Lab, advised by Prof. Kyoungwon Seo. I build the systems I study — multi-agent LLM systems, LLM-as-a-judge evaluation, VR tasks in Unity, multimodal behavioral models — for tutoring, debate, career counseling and early cognitive screening. The work runs on funded projects for the Ministry of Science and ICT, KERIS and Tutorus Labs, with clinical partners at Hanyang University Guri Hospital.
