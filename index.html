Human-Centered Agentic AI · Human–AI Interaction

Designing agentic AI that finds your blind spots instead of covering them.

I am Bogyeom Park, a Ph.D. student at SeoulTech’s Human-centered Artificial Intelligence Lab, advised by Prof. Kyoungwon Seo. I build the systems I study — multi-agent LLM pipelines, VR tasks in Unity, multimodal behavioral models — and test where the work should pass back from the agent to the person: tutoring, debate, career counseling, early cognitive screening.

Agentic Career-Counseling System

Virtual Kiosk Test

Ordering a meal, as a cognitive test. Built in Unity, told once with no reminders — and what the study reads is how the task is carried out, not whether it is finished. You can do the same six steps in your browser.

Self-Disclosing Chatbot

It tells you it cannot sleep before it asks whether you can. The control condition asked the same questions with the disclosures taken out, and that one difference changed what students noticed about their own stress. Talk to it, and it scores what you said against the inventory at the end.
