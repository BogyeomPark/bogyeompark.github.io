Human-Centered Agentic AI · Human–AI Interaction

Designing agentic AI that finds your blind spots instead of covering them.

I am Bogyeom Park, a Ph.D. student at SeoulTech’s Human-centered Artificial Intelligence Lab, advised by Prof. Kyoungwon Seo. I build multi-agent LLM systems and multimodal behavioral models, and test where the work should pass back from the agent to the person — in tutoring, debate, career counseling, and early cognitive screening.
