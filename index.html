Agentic AI · Human–AI Interaction

Designing agentic AI that finds your blind spots instead of covering them.

I am Bogyeom Park, a Ph.D. student at SeoulTech’s Human-centered Artificial Intelligence Lab (HAI Lab), advised by Prof. Kyoungwon Seo. Most AI fills a person’s gaps so smoothly they never see them. I build agents that surface those gaps rather than silently filling them, and study whether seeing what’s missing changes what someone can do. I explore this across tutoring, debate, career counseling, and early cognitive screening.
