Research

Human-centered Agentic AI

I design and study AI agents that reason and act with people—not simply for them. My work examines how agent behavior, interaction design, and evaluation can support human learning, accessibility, and decision-making.

Research Threads

01 · DESIGN

Human-Centered Agentic AI

Designing agents that can plan, use tools, and adapt their behavior while keeping human goals and oversight central.

02 · APPLICATION

Agentic AI for Education

Building educational agents that prompt learners to explain, compare, reflect, and develop their own reasoning.

03 · EVALUATION

Agent Evaluation & Analytics

Combining controlled experiments, qualitative inquiry, and interaction traces to understand what agents do and how people respond.
