Virtual Kiosk Test

You are given an order once. Then an unfamiliar menu, six steps, and nothing that reminds you what you came for. It takes about a minute — and it is the minute that told our JMIR study which participants had mild cognitive impairment. The screens are the study's own. At the end you get your numbers, and then the same numbers written up two ways, because a second study found that how a result is worded changes what the reader does next.
