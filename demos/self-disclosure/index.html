Demos / Self-disclosing chatbot

The chatbot goes first

Ask a student to describe their academic stress and you get a short answer. So this one talks about itself first, then asks.

Play it

It runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Every marked message is verbatim from the study’s protocol, so nothing here needs a model behind it.

Watch it

Both chatbots from the study, side by side on the same inventory. Each question lands on the two of them at once, so the answers can be read against each other.

What to know

The control chatbot ran the identical inventory with every disclosure removed. That single difference is the whole independent variable, and the video above is the two of them side by side.

A demonstration, not a screening tool. Nothing here assesses anyone.
