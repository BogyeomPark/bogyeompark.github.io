Demos / Agentic career-counseling system

Student records in, a report out

Career counseling asks a teacher to hold a student’s record, the admissions landscape and the conversation itself in mind at once. This system takes the first of those and leaves the rest alone. It reads the school records, drafts a comprehensive opinion, and shows which record each claim came from — so the teacher checks a source instead of trusting a paragraph. Fifty-two seconds, narrated. Designed and validated with teachers in our HCI Korea 2026 study.

The walkthrough

No sound is needed to follow it — every step is captioned on screen.

What it does

Four record types go in at once — creative activities, subject performance, behavior and opinion, detailed abilities — and school course data if the teacher has it. From there:

  • It reads the whole record. Automatic ingestion of school records was the highest-rated function in both validation rounds, and the part teachers described as costliest to do by hand.
  • It drafts the comprehensive opinion. The long-form judgment that a teacher would otherwise assemble from four files.
  • It shows its sources. Every claim in the draft points back to the record line it came from, so a teacher can check rather than trust.
  • It takes what the records missed. A teacher knows things no file contains; one click folds that in and the draft is rebuilt around it.

What stays with the teacher

The design separates two things that usually arrive together. Administrative load — reading, cross-referencing, drafting — is what the agent takes. Counseling judgment — what this student should hear, and when — stays where it was.

That split is why the traceability matters. A system that hands a teacher a finished opinion asks to be believed. This one hands over a draft with its sources attached, which is a different request: check me.

What the experts said

The system was built from focus group interviews with teachers and domain experts, then validated twice — once on the design direction, once on the working prototype.

  • 4.37 / 5 — expert rating of the design direction after the first round.
  • 5.00 / 5 — usefulness of automatic record ingestion, at a content validity index of 1.00. The highest-rated function in both rounds.
  • 2.75 / 5 — satisfaction with the creative-experiential-activity information. Experts were not convinced by this component, and it is reported here for the same reason it is reported in the paper.
