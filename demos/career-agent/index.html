Demos / Agentic career-counseling system

Student records in, a report out

Career counseling asks a teacher to hold three things at once: the record, the admissions landscape, and the conversation. This system takes the first and leaves the rest alone.

Watch it

The paper, HCI Korea 2026 →

What to know

The administrative load — reading four record files, cross-referencing them, drafting the comprehensive opinion — is what the agent takes. Counseling judgment stays with the teacher. That is why every claim carries its source: a finished opinion asks to be believed, a sourced draft asks to be checked.

Built from focus-group interviews with teachers, then validated twice. Automatic record ingestion rated 5.00 / 5; the creative-activity information rated 2.75 / 5, reported here as it is in the paper.
