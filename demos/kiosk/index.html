Demos / Virtual kiosk test

Ordering a meal, as a cognitive test

Using a kiosk asks more of you than it looks. Hold an order in memory, find each item on an unfamiliar menu, and act.

Play it

Your time and errors stay in the browser. Two anonymous counts leave it: a run started, a run finished.

The paper, JMIR 2024 →

Watch it

The task as the study ran it: the participant on the left, what they see on the right. Then the three signals it records, and what the model makes of them.

What to know

The study drew four biomarkers from this task. Time and errors survive the move to a browser; the other two do not. Hand movement speed needs a tracked controller, and scanpath length needs the eye tracker in the headset. That is where the impaired group’s eyes traveled furthest, which is why the study used VR.

A demonstration, not a screening test. Nothing here can diagnose anyone.
