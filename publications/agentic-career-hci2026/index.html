From Teacher Needs to Agentic AI: Designing and Validating a Personalized Career Counseling System

Bogyeom Park, Mina Yoo, Dongkuk Lee, Mi-ae Choi, Seona Park, So Young Jo, and Kyoungwon Seo

HCI Korea 2026 · Oral Presentation

Open PDF ↗
On this pageOverviewMethodKey FindingsContributionCitation

Overview

Career and college counseling asks a teacher to hold a student’s record, the admissions landscape and the conversation itself in mind at once. This work starts from the tasks teachers described as costly, and builds an agentic system around those needs rather than around what a model can generate.

Method

The study proceeded in three phases: focus group interviews with teachers and domain experts to establish needs, an expert validation survey of the resulting design, and a high-fidelity prototype built with Azure OpenAI (GPT-4o) and Streamlit, validated again by experts on usefulness, satisfaction and content validity.

Key Findings

4.37Expert rating of the design direction on a five-point scale after the first validation.
5.00Usefulness of automatic school-record ingestion, at a content validity index of 1.00 — the highest-rated function in both rounds.
2.75Satisfaction with creative-experiential-activity information, the one component experts were not convinced by.

Contribution

  • Derives the system from teacher-stated needs rather than fitting teachers to an existing tool.
  • Separates administrative load, which the agent can take, from counseling judgment, which stays with the teacher.
  • Reports the component that failed validation alongside those that passed.
