A Self-Determination Theory-Based Career Counseling Chatbot: Motivational Interactions to Address Career Decision-Making Difficulties and Enhance Engagement

Hyerim Han, Bogyeom Park, and Kyoungwon Seo

Overview

This work investigates whether motivational interactions grounded in self-determination theory can improve AI-supported career counseling. The chatbot design translates the needs for competence, relatedness, and autonomy into conversational support for career decision-making.

Method

Twenty university students used either the SDT-based chatbot or a general career-counseling chatbot. The study compared changes in career decision-making difficulties and examined engagement through four observed aspects: crystallization, self-reflection, revalidation, and epistemic curiosity.

Key Findings

4.54 → 4.14Career decision-making difficulty after the SDT chatbot (p < .001).
4.24 → 4.22The same measure after a general career chatbot (p = .86).
2 of 4Forms of engagement seen only with the SDT chatbot — revalidation and epistemic curiosity. Overall engagement did not differ.

Research Contribution

  • Translates self-determination theory into concrete motivational interactions for an AI career-counseling setting.
  • Shows stronger support for career decision-making difficulties than a general chatbot condition.
  • Identifies multiple qualitative forms of engagement while reporting that overall engagement did not differ significantly between conditions.
