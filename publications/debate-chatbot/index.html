Assessing Critical Thinking through a Multi-Agent LLM-Based Debate Chatbot

Bogyeom Park and Kyoungwon Seo

CHI EA '25 · Article 80 · Pages 1–13 · Published April 25, 2025

Read paperDownload PDFDOI ↗

Abstract

This work presents an LLM-based debate chatbot framework for assessing critical thinking across two complementary dimensions: argumentation skills and intellectual standards. It compares a single-agent evaluator with a multi-agent evaluator designed to mirror the way human experts consider different aspects of a debate before forming an overall judgment.

Key Results

0.78Intraclass Correlation Coefficient between the multi-agent model and human evaluators
97.37%Agreement within ±1 score between the multi-agent model and human evaluation
2 dimensionsArgumentation skills and intellectual standards assessed independently

Research Contribution

  • A multidimensional framework that combines structural argument analysis with qualitative standards of reasoning.
  • A multi-agent assessment process that more closely follows nuanced human assessment patterns than a single evaluator.
  • A scalable foundation for automated critical-thinking assessment in debate-based education.
