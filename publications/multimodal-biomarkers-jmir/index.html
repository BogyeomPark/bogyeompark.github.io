Integrating Biomarkers From Virtual Reality and Magnetic Resonance Imaging for the Early Detection of Mild Cognitive Impairment Using a Multimodal Learning Approach

Bogyeom Park, Yujin Kim, Jiwoo Park, Hyunbin Choi, Se Eun Kim, Hojin Ryu, and Kyoungwon Seo

Journal of Medical Internet Research · Vol. 26, e54538 · Published April 17, 2024

Overview

This validation study investigates whether behavioral biomarkers collected in a virtual kiosk task can complement structural MRI biomarkers for early detection of mild cognitive impairment. It connects interaction behavior from an everyday task simulation with clinical neuroimaging evidence.

Method

Data from 54 participants—22 healthy controls and 32 participants with MCI—were used to compare support vector machine models. The analysis evaluated VR-derived behavioral biomarkers, structural MRI biomarkers, and their multimodal integration.

Key Findings

94.4%Accuracy of the multimodal model integrating VR and MRI biomarkers
100%Sensitivity achieved by the integrated VR + MRI model
90.9%Specificity achieved by the integrated VR + MRI model

Research Contribution

  • Connects observable behavior in an everyday VR task with structural changes measured through MRI.
  • Shows complementary strengths: VR offers high specificity for screening, while MRI offers high sensitivity for confirmation.
  • Supports a sequential clinical strategy combining accessible behavioral screening with neuroimaging-based confirmation.
