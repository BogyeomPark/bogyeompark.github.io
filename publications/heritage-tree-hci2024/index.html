Artificial Intelligence-Based Heritage Tree Disease Diagnosis Using Transfer Learning: A Case Study of Zelkova serrata

Sabin Lee, Bogyeom Park, Daejung Kim, and Kyoungwon Seo

HCI Korea 2024 · Pages 212–219

Open PDF ↗Publisher ↗
On this pageOverviewMethodKey FindingsContributionCitation

Overview

Heritage trees are protected by law, but the monitoring that protection assumes is expensive enough that trees are lost every year to disease caught too late. More than half of South Korea’s designated protected trees are Zelkova serrata, which makes it the case worth solving first.

Method

A dataset of 680 Zelkova serrata images was labeled and then validated by a tree specialist with 29 years of diagnostic experience. Several state-of-the-art models were compared under transfer learning, first with ImageNet weights alone and then with additional pre-training on plant disease datasets, using label smoothing to handle uncertain cases.

Key Findings

99.45%Best F1 score once models were also pre-trained on plant disease data.
92.0–96.3%F1 range from ImageNet weights alone, before any domain pre-training.

Contribution

  • An expert-validated image dataset for a species that accounts for most of the country’s protected trees.
  • Suggests that domain-specific pre-training contributed more to the remaining gains than the choice of architecture.
  • Puts continuous monitoring within reach of the budgets that currently prevent it.
