Exploring the Multimodal Integration of VR and MRI Biomarkers for Enhanced Early Detection of Mild Cognitive Impairment

Bogyeom Park, Yuwon Kim, Jinseok Park, Hojin Choi, Seong-Eun Kim, Hokyoung Ryu, and Kyoungwon Seo

CHI EA '24 · Honolulu, USA · 8 pages · May 2024

Read paperDownload PDFDOI ↗

Abstract

This study compares VR-derived behavioral biomarkers and structural MRI biomarkers for mild cognitive impairment detection, then evaluates their integration in a multimodal support vector machine. The work examines how an accessible everyday-task simulation and clinical neuroimaging can contribute complementary evidence.

Key Results

94.4%Accuracy when VR and MRI biomarkers were integrated
100%Sensitivity of the combined multimodal model
90.9%Specificity of the combined multimodal model

Research Contribution

  • Demonstrates the value of behavioral biomarkers obtained from interaction with a virtual everyday task.
  • Identifies different diagnostic strengths of VR and MRI biomarkers.
  • Proposes VR-based initial screening followed by MRI-based confirmation as a practical sequential approach.
