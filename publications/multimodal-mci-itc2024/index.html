Multimodal Machine Learning Model for MCI Detection Using EEG, MRI and VR Data

Mariem Kallel, Bogyeom Park, Kyoungwon Seo, and Seong-Eun Kim

ITC-CSCC 2024 · Pages 1–6 · Published August 2024

Publisher ↗
On this pageOverviewCitation

Overview

Mild cognitive impairment is hard to catch early: the cognitive and structural brain changes it involves are subtle, and the safe, effective ways to look for them are limited. A virtual environment can put memory, attention and spatial awareness under load, MRI shows the structural change, and EEG picks up shifts in activity and connectivity without touching the patient. This paper builds a multimodal model that separates mild cognitive impairment from healthy controls using all three together.

This overview follows the published abstract, which does not report the model’s performance; the full text is available from the publisher and is not hosted on this site.
